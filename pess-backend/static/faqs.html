❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register?

Click the Register link on the login page and fill in your details. Students must provide accurate information. Parents can register using their child’s serial number or request the admin to create their account.

What if I forget my password?

If you forget your password, contact the system admin. For security reasons, passwords are not reset automatically — the admin will verify your identity and assist you in setting a new one.

Can parents access the system?

Yes. Parents log in using their child’s student serial number. If they don’t have it, they should ask the student directly or contact the admin to register them. This ensures parents are tied to the correct student record.

Are students beneficiaries of the system?

Yes. Students benefit directly by accessing the Virtual Library with books, home packages, past papers, and learning resources. They also receive notifications, track attendance, and monitor performance.

What can admins do?

Admins manage user accounts, upload resources, handle suspensions, post announcements, and oversee system content. They also assist parents and students with registration or password recovery.

Can visitors use PESS?

Visitors can explore public resources and learn how to register. Full features require an account.

Is my data secure?

Yes. PESS uses secure authentication and encrypted storage. Always log out after use for safety.

How do I contact support?

You can reach support via email at support@pess.com or phone at +255 700 123 456. Live chat is available weekdays 9 AM – 5 PM.
