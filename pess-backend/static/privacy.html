🔒 Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

PESS collects only the information necessary to operate the system effectively:

  • Login credentials (username/serial number and password).
  • Student academic records, attendance, and performance data.
  • Parent contact details for account linkage and communication.
2. Data Storage

All data is stored securely in encrypted databases. Access is restricted to authorized admins only.

3. Data Usage

Collected data is used strictly for educational and administrative purposes:

  • Tracking student progress and attendance.
  • Managing fees, suspensions, and announcements.
  • Providing parents with accurate updates linked to their child’s record.
4. Security Measures

PESS uses secure authentication and encrypted storage. Forgotten passwords require admin intervention with multi‑factor verification questions to confirm identity before reset.

5. Rights of Parents & Students

Parents and students have the right to:

  • Access their own records through secure login.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Contact the admin for account support or password recovery.
6. Admin Responsibilities

Admins are responsible for:

  • Verifying accounts during registration.
  • Assisting with password recovery using multi‑factor verification.
  • Ensuring data is not shared with third parties without consent.
7. Third‑Party Sharing

PESS does not share student or parent data with external parties unless required by law or with explicit consent.
